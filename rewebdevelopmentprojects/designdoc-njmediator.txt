INTRODUCTION SECTION
John F. Gelson has served as a mediator, trial attorney, business counsel, and legal advisor for more than four decades.
Since 1995, he has conducted more than 1,500 mediations across commercial, construction, employment, franchise, and complex business disputes.
Known for his practical judgment, calm demeanor, and ability to resolve difficult matters efficiently, John is trusted by attorneys, business leaders, insurance carriers, and private clients throughout New Jersey and New York.
